my experience

Where I've actually gotten my hands dirty.

Robotics Software Engineer

Ongoing

MCS Robotics · Stockholm, Sweden

Focused on the robotics and vision/perception side, including sensor fusion, deployment, model predictive control (MPC), and ROS control.

MCS Robotics builds autonomous systems that replace manual container cleaning, work that's traditionally slow, costly, and hazardous for workers. Their system, the Box Cleaner, cleans the inside of shipping containers with high-pressure water and air, and the company has partnered with Micropolis and the Port of Helsingborg to develop and test it.

The problem: LiDAR returns scatter off the airborne mist thrown up by the cleaning jets; ultrasound drowns in blower noise. There's no gap in the spray to sense around: the interference is constant.

My focus: Rather than fight the sensors the environment defeats, I'm building a 3D map of the container from LiDAR and a TP-Link camera in advance, so the robot can localize against known wall geometry instead of trusting live noisy returns. That's mcs_mapping, a ROS2 package with an ICP pipeline running on Jetson.

The stack: Hesai JT128 LiDAR, TP-Link camera, ICP, visual odometry with ORB feature matching, ROS2, Jetson.

Where it stands: Actively debugging mapping artifacts and exploring mmWave radar as a longer-term answer to the mist problem itself.
