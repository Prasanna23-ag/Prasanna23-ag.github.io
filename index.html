Mapping through mist

The problem. A robot cleans the inside of shipping containers with high-pressure water and air. LiDAR returns scatter off airborne mist; ultrasound drowns in blower noise. There's no gap in the spray to sense around: the interference is constant.

The approach. Rather than fight the sensors the environment defeats, I'm building a 3D map of the container from LiDAR and camera in advance, so the robot can localize against known wall geometry instead of trusting live noisy returns. That's mcs_mapping, a ROS2 package with an ICP pipeline running on Jetson.

The stack. Hesai JT128 LiDAR, ICP, visual odometry with ORB feature matching, ROS2, Jetson.

Where it stands. Actively debugging mapping artifacts and exploring mmWave radar as a longer-term answer to the mist problem itself.
